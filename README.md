# Retail Sales Analysis using SQL

## 1. Problem Statement

The business wants to understand:
• Who are the most valuable customers?
• Which segments drive revenue?
• Where are potential revenue losses happening?
Goal: Identify actionable insights to improve customer targeting and revenue.

## 2. Dataset

📌 Transactional retail dataset containing:
📌 Customer ID
📌 Purchase amount
📌 Order frequency
📌 Product/category details

## 3. Key Business Questions
📌 Which customers contribute the most revenue?
📌 Are there low-performing customer segments?
📌 Is revenue concentrated or evenly distributed?
📌 Are there early signs of customer churn?

## 4. Data Preparation & Validation
📌 Removed duplicate records to avoid double counting
📌 Handled missing values in customer and transaction fields
📌 Validated consistency of revenue calculations
📌 Ensured clean dataset before analysis

## 5. Analysis Performed
📌 Customer segmentation based on total revenue contribution
📌 Identification of high-value vs low-value customers
📌 Revenue distribution analysis using SQL aggregations
📌 Frequency analysis to identify repeat vs one-time buyers

## 6. Key Insights
📌 A small percentage of customers contribute a majority of total revenue
📌 High-value customers show consistent purchase behavior
📌 Low-value customers have low engagement and higher churn risk
📌 Revenue is highly concentrated, indicating dependency on a small segment

## 7. Business Impact
📌 Focus retention strategies on high-value customers
📌 Introduce targeted campaigns to convert low-value customers into repeat buyers
📌 Reduce dependency risk by expanding mid-tier customer base
📌 Personalize offers based on purchase behavior

## 8. Tools Used
SQL (Joins, Aggregations, Window Functions)
